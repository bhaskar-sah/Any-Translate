# Privacy Policy for Any-Translate

**Last Updated: October 2026**

**Any-Translate** ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by **Bhaskar Sah**, provides an offline and online multilingual dictionary and translation mobile application. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, usage, and disclosure.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

### 1.1 Offline Lookups & Translations
Any-Translate is built with a privacy-first, on-device architecture:
- Single-word dictionary lookups, definitions, and pronunciations are processed entirely locally using an internal SQLite database stored on your device.
- On-device AI translations using Google ML Kit execute locally without transmitting your personal text inputs to any remote server.
- Search history and bookmarked favorites are saved locally within your private application storage space.

### 1.2 Network Features & Third-Party Services
For extended queries and monetization, the app utilizes the following standard services:

1. **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)**:
   - To keep this application 100% free for users, we integrate Google AdMob to serve advertisements.
   - AdMob may collect and process information such as your device identifier (Advertising ID), IP address, general approximate location, and interaction with ads to serve non-personalized or personalized advertising in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
   - For more details, review [Google's Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

2. **Online Fallback Translation API**:
   - Queries not resolved in the offline database may optionally use public translation APIs over secure HTTPS connections. No personally identifiable account data is attached to translation queries.

---

## 2. Permissions Requested

- `INTERNET`: Required to download on-device language models, fetch extended translations when online, and load AdMob advertisements.
- `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: Required to check network connectivity status before initiating downloads.

---

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion

All user data (such as search history and saved bookmarks) resides strictly on the user's device. You can clear your search history at any time from within the app using the "Clear History" button, or by uninstalling the application.

---

## 4. Children's Privacy

Our application does not knowingly address or collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
